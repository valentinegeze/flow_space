Kinect depth median-smoothed over 10 consecutive frames per timestamp, projected to 3D, rotated so the dry-bed plane lies horizontal, and binned into a top-down grid at 1.0 mm/cell. Output is sim-ready: each cell has a known mm² area and a bed-relative height in mm. The same transform is applied to all timestamps so heightmaps are directly comparable.

trajectory
Median Δheight vs time · with 5/95 percentile band
Bed change accumulates in the first 60 s (scour as the channel cuts down), peaks near −2 mm median at t = 90 s, then partially refills to ≈ −1 mm steady state as upstream sediment loads in. The percentile band widens with time — local scour pockets get deeper than the median while shallow-deposition shoulders remain near zero.

handoff to phase 3
How these maps feed the post-fire flood model
Two products go forward: (a) the channel network mask from the optical-flow stream signature (where flow lingered) — input to the per-cell perturbation in burn-perturbation.js (∈ [0,1] severity map). (b) the rectified bed at t=210s (a real measured surface, not a synthetic DEM) — input to the next session's burn-scar reconstruction where particle-size collapse under fire is modeled against this measured initial topography.
